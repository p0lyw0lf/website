## xaev – [berdlycore chapter 3](https://xaev.bandcamp.com/album/berdlycore-chapter-3)

i love hyperflip so much u must understand. the nostalgiabait is targeted exactly at my age, the chaotic beats are exactly what i need for some good overstim. there is not a whole lot specifically deltarune-themed about this, go listen to the OST if u want that, this is for PRO GAMERS ONLY 😤

## D-CELL GAMES – [unbeatable [ost]](https://dcellgames.bandcamp.com/album/unbeatable-ost)

Friend shared this game with me a while ago. It's a really good rhythm game with really good music and a really good story, you beat up a bunch of cops, it's great, you should play it. [UNBEATABLE on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2240620/UNBEATABLE/)

![](https://static.wolfgirl.dev/polywolf/blog/019fc377-a36f-7cc9-b766-f66e5e88434f/unbeatable.jpg)

Favorite track: ZERO MOMENT (plays near the climax, spoiler-free tho)

<iframe style="border: 0; width: 100%; height: 120px;" src="https://bandcamp.com/EmbeddedPlayer/album=3703015056/size=large/bgcol=ffffff/linkcol=0687f5/tracklist=false/artwork=small/track=3673190905/transparent=true/" seamless><a href="https://dcellgames.bandcamp.com/album/unbeatable-ost">unbeatable [ost] by D-CELL SOUND TEAM</a></iframe>

Don't let my choice of "harsh electronica" fool you tho; there is a lot of prog rock on here too. Wide range of stuff to suite any palette.

Also there is [Jaime Paige DLC](https://dcellgames.bandcamp.com/album/the-jamie-paige-content-companions-official-companion-content) for this game, has some absolutely insane remixes of classic JaimeP songs, highly recommend that as well :3
